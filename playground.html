      Limitations
      • This is a tiny model — 45M parameters for v2, 26M for v1. Expect mistakes, ignored commands, and odd rewrites. It is a tech demo, not a reliable assistant.
      • Edits are limited to text and inline styles of elements visible in this tab. Anything marked data-no-edit is off-limits, and no changes persist after a refresh.
      • The model only sees the current DOM — it has no browsing, memory across runs, or knowledge of the world beyond what's on this page.
      • English prompts only. Runs entirely on your device's CPU/GPU, so latency depends on your hardware.
